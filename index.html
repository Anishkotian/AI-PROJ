An AI Strategy Game

SAVE THE VILLAIN

The hero is coming. Only you can stop him.

How to Play

  • Goal: keep the villain alive until the countdown (in turns) reaches zero.
  • Move your assistant with the arrow keys or WASD.
  • Pick a tool (Trap, Obstacle, Door) and click a tile in range. Keys 1, 2, 3 also select tools.
  • Each turn you have a few actions. Press End Turn (or E) and the hero moves one tile.
  • Traps make the hero lose turns when he steps on them, then the trap is used up.
  • Doors can be opened or closed. The hero recalculates and looks for another route.
  • You cannot seal the villain in. The game refuses a move that leaves no route.
  • Green line: the hero's planned path. Orange dashed: his previous path. Blue wash: explored nodes.
  • You lose when the hero reduces the villain's health to zero.

AI Algorithms

A* Search

f(n) = g(n) + h(n)

g(n): cost already paid from the start. h(n): estimated cost to the goal (Manhattan distance). f(n): total estimated cost. It always expands the lowest f, avoids expensive traps when a cheap detour exists, and finds the shortest path.

Greedy Best-First Search

f(n) = h(n)

Only looks at the estimated distance to the goal. Often explores fewer nodes, but ignores cost already paid, so it can walk into traps and return longer paths.
